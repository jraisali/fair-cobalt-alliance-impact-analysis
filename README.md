# Fair Cobalt Alliance — Impact Analysis

Econometric project evaluating the potential impact of the **Fair Cobalt Alliance (FCA)** around Kamilombe in the Democratic Republic of Congo.

## Research question

The analysis focuses on two outcomes:

- Education levels among individuals aged 15–25
- Household access to improved water sources

## Data

The project uses three waves of DHS survey data:

- 2007
- 2013
- 2023

## Methodology

The analysis is based on a **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** approach comparing areas located near Kamilombe with areas located farther away.

The project also includes:

- Treatment and control group construction
- Event-study specification
- Parallel trends assessment
- Robustness checks using different distance thresholds
- Interpretation of statistical significance and methodological limitations

## Main findings

The analysis did not identify a statistically significant causal effect of the FCA on education or access to improved water at the regional level.

The results should be interpreted cautiously due to several limitations, including the geographic scale of the analysis and the relatively short period between the launch of the FCA and the latest survey wave.
